## How to Use

- Read sequentially from Level 1, or jump to the level you need through the index;
- Each chapter first states the problem and the interface, then gives the dual-language implementations;
- Each chapter includes normal cases, boundary cases, complexity analysis and exercises;
- The glossary is there to look up abbreviations and their full English names.
